A workflow is a chain of API calls where each step can use data from the previous ones. You build it in the **Design** tab by dragging operations onto a canvas, and Luvin writes the file for you.

<video controls muted playsinline preload="metadata" poster="/media/en/design-workflow.webp" src="/media/en/design-workflow.mp4"></video>

## Example: Pokemon card

**Pokemon card** has four steps. The first gets a Pokémon from the input `pokemon`. The other three use what it returned: its `id`, its main type and its first ability.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="In the app">

The canvas shows **Start** with the input `pokemon`, then four cards joined by arrows, then **End of workflow**. The second card, `getSpecies`, shows the parameter `idOrName` with the source `getPokemon › id`.

![The Design tab with the operations palette on the left, the canvas with wired steps in the centre and the step panel on the right](/media/en/builder.webp)

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="In the YAML">

```yaml title="pokemon.arazzo.yaml" {6,8,15}
- stepId: getPokemon
  operationId: $sourceDescriptions.pokeapi.getPokemon
  parameters:
    - name: idOrName
      in: path
      value: $inputs.pokemon
  outputs:
    id: $response.body#/id
    mainType: $response.body#/types/0/type/name
- stepId: getSpecies
  operationId: $sourceDescriptions.pokeapi.getPokemonSpecies
  parameters:
    - name: idOrName
      in: path
      value: $steps.getPokemon.outputs.id
```

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## What a workflow is made of

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Step** | One call: an operation of a contract, a saved request, a message or another workflow. |
| **Input** | A value the workflow asks for when it runs, for example the name of a Pokémon. |
| **Output** | A value a step takes from its response so the next steps can use it. |
| **Success criterion** | The rule that says a step went well, for example "it responds 200". |
| **Action** | What happens when a step answers with an error: end, retry or go to another step. |

A *contract* is the OpenAPI or AsyncAPI file that describes an API. The [glossary](/reference/glossary/) has every term.

## Open the Design tab

<Steps>
1. In the sidebar, click a workflow.
2. Click the **Design** tab.
</Steps>

The canvas shows the workflow from top to bottom. **Design** is the tab a workflow opens on, except in the Simple interface, which opens on **Overview**.

## The parts of the Design tab

| Area | Where | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **Operations** palette | Left | Everything you can add: the operations of each contract, **Collection requests**, **Reusables** and **Workflows in this Arazzo**. Search, drag or press **+**. The arrow in its header hides it. |
| Canvas | Centre | The workflow as cards joined by arrows: **Start** with the inputs, one card per step, and **End of workflow** with the outputs. |
| Step panel | Right | Opens when you click a card. Here you edit parameters, body, success, errors and outputs. Click an empty spot of the canvas to close it. |
| Toolbar | Above the canvas | **Undo**, **Redo** and **+ Workflow**. |

Drag the dividers between the three areas to resize them. Double-click a divider to go back to the original size.

### What a step card shows

- Its position in the run and its name. Point at the card to see the arrows **↑** and **↓** that move it.
- **modifies data** when the operation is a POST, PUT, PATCH or DELETE.
- The method and path of the operation, and the contract it comes from.
- **Parameters**: each value and where it comes from. `pokemon` is an input, `getPokemon › id` is an output of another step, and anything else is a fixed value.
- **Success criteria** and **Outputs**.
- **On failure**: what happens on each error.

### Show: the two layers

Above the canvas, **Show:** has two switches.

| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Actions by response** | Adds the **On failure** section to each card and draws the jumps to other steps. The number is how many actions the workflow defines. |
| **Data flow** | The number is how many connections carry data from one step to another. In the diagram of the **Overview** tab it shows or hides those lines. In **Design** the data lines are always drawn, because that is where you edit them. |

To the right, the legend explains the dots: green is **success** and orange is **jumps to another step**.

### Move around the canvas

- Drag the background to move. Use the buttons in the corner to zoom in, zoom out and fit the whole workflow in view.
- Drag a card or the vertical part of a line to tidy the drawing. **Reset layout** appears above the canvas and puts everything back.

<Aside type="note">
Moving a card sideways only changes the drawing. Moving it above or below another step changes the order of the run. See [Organize a workflow](/design/organize/).
</Aside>
