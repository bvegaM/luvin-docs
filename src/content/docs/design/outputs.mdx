An output is a value a step takes from its response and gives a name to, so the next steps can use it. In the Pokédex, the step `getPokemon` has the outputs `id`, `name` and `mainType`, and the following steps read them. This page shows how to add outputs to a step and to the workflow.

## Example: the outputs of getPokemon

In **Pokemon card**, `getPokemon` keeps five values. Later steps read `id`, `mainType` and `firstAbility`.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="In the app">

Under **Outputs** in the step panel, the fields `id`, `name`, `types/type/name`, `abilities/ability/name` and `base_experience` are ticked. On the card, each output has a dot you can drag to a later parameter.

![The step panel with the response fields ticked as outputs and the action chosen for each error](/media/en/step-outputs.webp)

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="In the YAML">

```yaml title="pokemon.arazzo.yaml" {2-6}
outputs:
  id: $response.body#/id
  name: $response.body#/name
  mainType: $response.body#/types/0/type/name
  firstAbility: $response.body#/abilities/0/ability/name
  baseExperience: $response.body#/base_experience
```

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## When you need to add one

Often you do not. When you connect a parameter to a field of an earlier response, Luvin adds the output to that step for you. See [Connect data between steps](/design/wire-data/).

Add one by hand when you want the workflow to return the value at the end, or to have it ready before you add the step that will use it.

## Tick the fields a step exposes

<Steps>
1. In the **Design** tab, click the step.
2. Go to **Outputs**, at the bottom of the panel. It says **What this step exposes to the next ones (from its response).**
3. Tick a field.
</Steps>

<video controls muted playsinline preload="metadata" poster="/media/en/design-outputs.webp" src="/media/en/design-outputs.mp4"></video>

The output appears on the card, under **Outputs**, with a dot on its right that you can drag to a parameter of a later step. Untick the field to remove the output.

The panel lists the fields of the success response the contract documents: lists first, then simple values such as texts, numbers and yes/no. The name of the output comes from the path of the field:

| Field | Output name |
|---|---|
| `id` | `id` |
| `customer/id` | `customerId` |
| `types/type/name` | `typesTypeName` |

If the panel says **The contract doesn't document the response: add outputs in the YAML.**, Luvin has no fields to offer. Write the output by hand as shown below.

<Aside type="caution">
If you untick an output that a later step uses, that step loses its value and shows an error. Give it another source, or click **Undo**.
</Aside>

## Fields that are lists

Responses often contain lists: the cards of a customer, the items of an order. The panel offers three kinds of fields for them.

| What you tick | Tag | What the output holds |
|---|---|---|
| The list itself, for example `cards` | **whole list** | All the items. |
| A field of the items, for example `cards/bank` | None | The value of one item. You choose which item when you run. |
| One value of a list of simple values, for example `tags` | **one item** | One of the values. You choose which when you run. |

Fields of list items are shown without a position: `cards/bank`, not `cards/0/bank`. When a step has outputs of this kind, the panel adds a note, **These outputs come from one item of “cards”: you choose which one when you run.**, with the button **Expose the whole “cards” list** in case you want all the items instead.

The choice happens during the run: Luvin pauses and shows what came back. See [Choose an item from a list](/test/lists/).

![The outputs of listProducts: items with the whole list tag, items/sku ticked, and the note with the button Expose the whole “items” list](/media/en/design-list-outputs.webp)

## Outputs written in the YAML

The panel covers fields picked by their path. An output can also be a search inside the response, written in the [YAML tab](/design/arazzo-editor/). In **Pokemon card**, the step `getAbility` keeps the English description of an ability with a JSONPath search:

```yaml
outputs:
  effect:
    context: $response.body
    selector: $.effect_entries[?(@.language.name == 'en')].short_effect
    type: jsonpath
```

An output like this appears in the panel below the fields, with its name, its expression and **Delete**. It works like any other output: later steps can use it, and it has a dot on the card.

## What the workflow returns

A workflow has its own outputs: what it returns when it ends. Other workflows that depend on it read them, and so does the report. You set them on the **End of workflow** card, the last one on the canvas.

<Steps>
1. Click **End of workflow**. The panel shows **Workflow outputs**.
2. Tick what the workflow should return. You can choose among the outputs of its steps (shown as `getPokemon › name`), the outputs of the workflows it depends on, and its inputs (shown as `input · pokemon`).
3. Each one you tick appears in the list below with a name. To change the name, click it, type and press Enter. The bin removes it.
</Steps>

You can also drag from the dot of any output to the **End of workflow** card.

If the panel says **Nothing to expose yet: declare outputs on the steps.**, add outputs to a step first.

For example, **Pokemon card** returns six values taken from its four steps, among them `name` and `mainType` from `getPokemon` and `weakTo` from `getWeakness`. On the canvas, a dashed line goes from each of those outputs to **End of workflow**.
