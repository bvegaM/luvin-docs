The guided tour walks you through Luvin on a real example, one highlighted area of the screen at a time. It takes about 6 minutes. Take it after the [quick start](/get-started/quick-start/) if you want to see every feature once.

![The guided tour highlighting the results of a run, with an explanation card next to it](/media/en/tour.webp)

## What the tour covers

It uses the Pokédex example against the public PokéAPI, which needs no credentials. At the end it shows a payment with PayPal, to explain authorization and reusables. In order, it covers:

- The workspace, **Home**, and the sidebar with collections, workflows and steps.
- The diagram of a workflow, its inputs and its environments.
- The generated cases, and a run against the real API.
- One step in detail, the YAML editor and a contract open next to its preview.
- A workflow that depends on another one, and a workflow that mixes an HTTP call with messaging steps.
- The PayPal example: reusables, authorization per API, and a run in **Simulation**.
- The **Design** tab, standalone requests, Dev Tools, search, the collection overview and Preferences.

You can use the app at any time while the tour is open. The tour does not block it.

## Start the tour

Use any of these:

| Where | What to click |
|---|---|
| Welcome screen | The **Guided tour** card |
| Status bar, at the bottom | **Tour** |
| Preferences (the gear icon in the status bar) | **Start the tour** |
| The banner **You’re viewing an example** | **Start the tour** |

See [A tour of the interface](/get-started/interface/) for the status bar and Preferences.

## The banner that offers the tour

When you open an example, a banner appears at the top of the screen: **You’re viewing an example.** *Want us to walk you through it? The tour covers every feature in about 6 minutes.*

- Click **Start the tour** to begin.
- Click the **✕** to close it. Its tooltip says **Don’t show again**. Luvin remembers it on this computer, so the banner does not come back for any example.
- The banner does not appear while the tour is running.

The tour stays available from the other places in the table above.

## What the tour creates

When it starts, the tour says **Getting ready…** and builds its own workspace:

<Steps>

1. If you have a workspace open, Luvin closes it.
2. Luvin creates a workspace called **Pokédex (tour)**. It is kept in the storage of the browser (or of the desktop app), not in a folder on disk.
3. It adds a collection, **Pokédex**, with the example files.

</Steps>

Later steps add a second collection, **PayPal**. The tour does its work inside **Pokédex (tour)**.

<Aside type="note">
The tour runs the example by itself, as you would: it opens a workflow, a tab, and presses **Run**. The run at the **Run against the real API** step calls pokeapi.co, so you need an internet connection.
</Aside>

## Move through the tour

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Next step | **Next** or the → key |
| Previous step | **Back** or the ← key |
| Leave the tour | **Exit tour** |

The card shows **Step 3 of N** and a progress bar. While a step is getting the screen ready, the card shows **Getting ready…**.

## When the tour ends

The last card is titled **All set!** and asks what to do with the **Pokédex (tour)** workspace:

| Button | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Keep it** | The workspace stays. Find it later under **Open a workspace** on the welcome screen |
| **Delete it** | The workspace is deleted, with its collections and its secrets. This cannot be undone |

You can run the tour again at any time. Each time it builds a fresh **Pokédex (tour)** workspace.

<Aside type="caution">
**Exit tour** closes the tour on the spot. It does not ask about the workspace, so **Pokédex (tour)** stays. To remove it later, open its menu in the top bar and choose **Delete workspace…**.
</Aside>
