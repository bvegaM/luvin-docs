Use **Akamai EdgeGrid** when the API is behind Akamai and its documentation asks for an EdgeGrid signature. The app marks the option **Akamai EdgeGrid (Beta)**.

## Example

```text
Authorization: EG1-HMAC-SHA256 client_token=akab-xxxx;access_token=akab-yyyy;timestamp=20261117T14:03:22+0000;nonce=<uuid>;signature=…
```

Timestamp and nonce are new on every send.

## Fields

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| **Client Token** | From your Akamai credential. A secret field. |
| **Client Secret** | From your Akamai credential. A secret field. |
| **Access Token** | From your Akamai credential. A secret field. |
| **Headers to sign** | Optional. Header names separated by commas. |
| **Max body size (bytes)** | Optional. `131072` when empty. |

## What Luvin signs

The method, the scheme, the host, the path with its query, the headers you listed (only those the request has), a hash of the body and the timestamp. The body enters the signature only for a `POST`, and only its first **Max body size** bytes.

It replaces an `Authorization` header you wrote yourself. The signature is calculated when sending.

## Secrets

**Client Token**, **Client Secret** and **Access Token** are all secrets: typed as-is they are stored encrypted on your computer and not in the file. See [Secrets](/authorization/secrets/).
