Use **WSSE Auth** when a SOAP or legacy service asks for a WS-Security *UsernameToken*: a username and a password digest instead of the password.

## Example

**Type** `WSSE Auth`, **Username** `ana`, **Password** `secret`. Luvin adds two headers:

```text
X-WSSE: UsernameToken Username="ana", PasswordDigest="…", Nonce="…", Created="2026-11-17T14:03:22.000Z"
Authorization: WSSE profile="UsernameToken"
```

`PasswordDigest` is the base64 of the SHA-1 of the nonce, the creation date and the password. The nonce and the date are new on every send.

## Fields

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| **Username** | The user. |
| **Password** | The password. A secret field. |

## Rules

- It replaces an `Authorization` header you wrote yourself.
- The digest is calculated when sending. In **Headers** the row reads *Luvin calculates it when sending (WSSE)*.

## Secrets

**Password** is a secret: stored encrypted on your computer when typed as-is. **Username** goes to the file. See [Secrets](/authorization/secrets/).
